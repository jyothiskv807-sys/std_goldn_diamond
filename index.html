Born in Payyavoor, Dedicated to Elegance

Founded with a commitment to unyielding purity and warm Malabar hospitality, Standard Gold & Diamonds (SFJ) opened its showroom doors in the heart of Payyavoor, Kannur.

We recognized that choosing jewellery is more than a purchase—it is an intimate emotion, an investment in generations, and the sparkle of lifelong memories. Our boutique showroom combines transparent gold testing with attentive, patient customer care.
